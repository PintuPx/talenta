# Talenta privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026 · applies to the current version of the Android app_

Talenta is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Summary
- Your data stays on your device. The app has **no internet permission** and cannot send your data anywhere.
- No account or registration is needed.
- There are no ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools in the app.

## What the app stores
The transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, repeating transactions, bills, bill photos and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

## Bill photos and text reading
You can take a photo of a bill with your camera app, or pick one with the system photo picker. The app needs no camera or storage permission. The photo is copied into the app's private storage and re-saved without its original metadata (such as location). The text on the bill is read **on your device** by Google ML Kit's built-in text recognition model; photos are never uploaded. ML Kit's usage-statistics component is removed from the app.

## Backups and exports
Backups (JSON) and transaction exports (CSV) are created only when you ask, and saved where you choose. These files are **not encrypted**: anyone with the file can read it. Bill photos are not included in backups. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual records in the app, use **Settings → Clear all data**, or uninstall the app to remove everything from your device.

## Children
The app is not directed at children.

## Changes
If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, adding optional sign-in), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
